# EV Charging Infrastructure Briefing

## Current US Public Charging Landscape

As of 2025, the United States has approximately **195,874 public charging ports** across **69,679 stations**, representing more than a sixfold increase from approximately 30,000 public chargers in 2016. This rapid expansion reflects growing EV adoption and increased infrastructure investment from federal initiatives like the 2021 Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act.

Key statistics:
- **California leads** with 49,128 public chargers, triple the second-place state (New York with 15,890)
- **65% of Americans** live in counties with at least one public charging station
- **Urban areas** have the highest accessibility: 60% of urban residents live within one mile of a charger, compared to 17% of rural Americans
- The nation has roughly **145,000 gasoline stations** for comparison

## Major Charging Networks for Fleet Operations

### ChargePoint
- **Scale**: Largest US charging network with nearly 43,000 locations
- **Reliability**: Established since 2007, backed by extensive federal ARRA funding
- **Fleet Advantages**: Comprehensive nationwide coverage, diverse charging speed options, proven reliability for commercial operations

### EVgo  
- **Specialization**: Focuses exclusively on DC fast charging stations
- **Growth**: Currently operates in 60 metropolitan areas across 30 states, planning to triple in size over five years
- **Fleet Benefits**: Fast charging technology ideal for minimizing downtime, customer-first features with reliability and special offers

**Alternative Major Networks:**
- **Tesla Superchargers**: High-speed network originally developed for Tesla vehicles, now expanding to other manufacturers
- **Electrify America**: Significant national network with strong highway corridor coverage
- **Blink**: Established network with both urban and highway charging locations

## Fleet Planning Considerations

For fleet operations, the combination of ChargePoint's extensive coverage and EVgo's fast-charging focus provides reliable redundancy. Both networks offer nationwide presence with strategic placement along major highways and in metropolitan areas, ensuring consistent service for commercial electric vehicle operations.
